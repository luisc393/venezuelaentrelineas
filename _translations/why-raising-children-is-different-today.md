*Give me a "why", and I'll find you the "hows".*

**1.** Because although oranges are still oranges, the means of transport have changed so much over the years that today we would not accept the quality of an orange carried on a donkey's back and left out in the open. Although children are always children, the means we parents have to carry them from childhood to maturity let us aim for better quality.

**2.** Because the sciences advance, change and update themselves, and in that process they keep concluding that what gives the best results is the best process, and behind it, the person carrying it out.

**3.** Because the human sciences (psychology, pedagogy, sociology) keep discovering truths about human beings that help us understand them and bring out the best in them.

**4.** Because human beings grow up without an instruction manual, and it is up to each of us to discover it. As we lean on the sciences to enlighten our understanding, we find answers to life's big questions more accurately, and in time for ourselves and our children.

**5.** Because raising children by intuition, or because "that's how they did it with me", is not enough when the world we grew up in is aggressively different from today's. Back then, at 11 you sailed paper boats in the bathtub. Today at 11 you surf the Internet... and play with fire.

**6.** Because it has been shown that genetic inheritance plays a very small part in forming habits and virtues compared with the large influence of learning, and so labels like "as tidy as Mom!!!" or "as late as Dad!!!" fall away: they did NOT inherit that... they learned it!!!

**7.** Because the neurosciences show us that 90% of the brain develops before the age of nine, and those years are the "golden age of education": either you got there in time or you didn't!!!

**8.** Because in education it is better to arrive a year early than a day late.

**9.** Because being ineffective discredits me and I lose authority. Because in the past one look from our parents was an order; today it is the children who glare at us to make us be quiet.

**10.** Because in this competitive society my greatest contribution will be to send into it young people who are consistent and whole.

**11.** Because in the past it was enough for a mother to know about cooking, nursing, organizing and upkeep. Today we must also know about planning, strategy, management, leadership, motivation, Theory Z, total quality, and so on and so on.

**12.** Because I want them to be happy, and to know where to look for happiness you must first have found it.

**13.** Because in the past communication was face to face, and today, between text messages, chat, Messenger and e-mail, information slips through our fingers and we cannot catch data in mid-air. Educating them in freedom is more important than ever, because the decision of what they see and what they write is theirs alone.

**14.** Because in the old days "DON'T talk to strangers" was a golden rule of personal safety; today they can talk to any stranger, at any hour, anywhere in the world. Only a well-formed will chooses prudence.

**15.** Because amid the constant bombardment of pornography, education in the emotions and in love is more necessary than ever.

**16.** Because if we are successful, up-to-date and productive professionals, why not also be parents with the same achievements?

*Luisana Graterón de Bethencourt, family counselor at IPEF*
