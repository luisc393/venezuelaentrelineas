**We all know that Venezuela has natural resources unlike any in the world: our coasts, our beaches... rivers... jungles... plains... mountains... wildlife... plants... mines... oil... but our greatest resource is ourselves: the Venezuelan people.**

*And above all, it is time for us to reflect seriously that life is a common good that must be shared.*

Today more than ever, Venezuela needs everyone's participation and commitment, because one in three Venezuelans is poorly fed, lives in precarious conditions and lacks basic services such as water, health care and education.

Many of us are convinced that solving our problems is everyone's responsibility. That is why we cannot stand by with folded arms in the face of the injustice our country is living through. We must and we can take part: let us live out social solidarity. Many of us, within our means, can help resolve situations of hunger, of physical or moral need, of exclusion, of violence, of human rights abuses.

We cannot allow, not for one more day, the passivity that comes from thinking we can do nothing, or very little, to paralyze us when we try to commit ourselves and take part. Small individual acts of generosity multiply and grow when they come together: one contribution adds to another, one grain of sand to another, one brotherly glance to thousands of glances.

The time has come for all of us, together, to take part in building a better country. How do we begin? What should we do? How can we channel our concerns, our dreams, our commitment to work for development, so as to achieve a better Venezuela?

Let us get to know our great allies, who can help us practice solidarity. Let us get to know those who, for many years, in silence and without rest, have worked with children, young people, individuals and communities, with the sole aim of contributing to the full development and well-being of everyone alike. Let us learn from the work of foundations, associations and NGOs, from their generosity, their dedication, their professionalism. Let us join them and work responsibly as volunteers; let us offer our work and our resources, and share our time, enthusiasm and experience. It is time to think and act for an "us"; it is the hour of the other.

It is time to give part of our life to the service of others. It is time for us to encourage one another. It is time for us to unite, to show solidarity, and to draw many more people toward the good.

Because Venezuela needs us today more than ever. Because many of us have woken up and want to join in the work for a better country. Because many of us are building a better Venezuela. Because many of us are dreaming the same things.

And is there any better dream than working for a better future through education, development and equal opportunity?

We invite you to walk with us, rediscovering the potential we all have, to achieve a better Venezuela.
